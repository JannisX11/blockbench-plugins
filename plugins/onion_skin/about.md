# Onion Skin

Semi-transparent "ghosts" of the model on neighboring keyframes — helps you refine motion arcs. Red = previous keyframes, green = next.

## Usage

Menu "Animation" → "Onion Skin (on/off)". Ghosts are drawn relative to the playhead and update when you scrub or edit keyframes; during playback they freeze in place (not rebuilt) but stay visible. Settings (how many frames before/after, opacity) — "Onion Skin — settings".
